# What users complain about

From 2,696 reviews of ABC Trainerize, TrueCoach and Everfit, read 11 October 2026:

- **Google Play:** the newest 2,514 from Australia, the US and the UK, including both coach apps.
- **App Store:** the 182 most helpful from the US, Australia, the UK and Canada.

The 1,579 that were 3 stars or lower, or named a problem, were sorted into pain points. Counts are reviews that raise each point; the number in brackets is how many are from 2025 or 2026. Store reviews are mostly from clients, so coach complaints also come from Capterra, G2 and Trustpilot (see `competitors.md`). Quotes are as written.

Every pain point below shows up in all three apps.

## The pain points, worst first

### P1. Logged workouts get lost
149 mentions (35). Blocks use.
> "You go to hit the done button and it spins and spins. doesn't save and then erases all of the data." (Trainerize)

**Infinilift:** each set saves on the phone the moment it's entered and syncs when there's signal. There's no Save step that can fail. A workout in progress survives switching apps, locking the screen, a crash or a restart, and reopens on the same set. Logged numbers can be fixed afterwards.
**Already does part of it:** TrainHeroic (offline mode, edit after logging), Hevy (workout survives a crash).

### P2. New people can't get in
155 mentions (36). Blocks use.
> "There isn't even a create account option when first getting the app, so its impossible to use" (Everfit)

**Infinilift:** the first screen after install offers "I have an invite or code", "Find my coach" and "I'm a coach". There's no dead-end login. Sign in with Apple, Google or a one-time code, with no passwords to reset. In a coach's branded app, the first screen is their sign-up page.
**Already does it:** TrainHeroic (three ways in after sign-up), Hevy (anyone can sign up, then accept an invite), QuickCoach (no password, a personal link).

### P3. Slow, crashes, useless without signal
125 mentions (43). Blocks use at the gym.
> "This wouldn't be an issue if my workout was accessible offline." (TrueCoach)

**Infinilift:** today's workout opens from the phone in under 2 seconds, with or without signal. The next 14 days of the program and the client's history are kept on the phone. When the network is slow, the app says the sets are saved and will send later. Public status page.
**Already does part of it:** TrainHeroic (offline mode, status page).

### P4. Updates break things and take features away
165 mentions (40).
> "Glorified message only app after the update - no workouts!!" (Everfit)

**Infinilift:** most fixes ship over the air, so nobody waits for a store download before training. The app only forces an update for security fixes. Updates go out in stages and roll back if crashes or failed saves rise. Layout changes are opt-in first and never remove a feature without notice. Dark mode and readable contrast from day one.
**Already does part of it:** PT Distinction (its 2026 redesign removed nothing).

### P5. Logging takes too many taps
191 mentions (64), the most recent complaints of any point.
> "I need to touch the screen 7 times to log my morning elliptical workout. Every morning." (Trainerize)

**Infinilift:** one scrolling screen shows every exercise, with an optional one-at-a-time mode. Each set is a row of typed fields filled in with what the coach prescribed. The number keypad moves from weight to reps to the next set, and one tap ticks a set. Clients can swap (coach-approved swaps first), reorder, add or skip, and move a workout to another day.
**Already does it:** Hevy (set rows), TrainHeroic (coach-approved swaps, rescheduling), FitPros.io (focus mode, one-tap fill).

### P6. Health data arrives wrong, late or not at all
318 mentions (50), the biggest cluster.
> "my Samsung Health shows 8k steps while the app shows 14k." (Trainerize)

**Infinilift:** Apple Health and Health Connect from launch, which brings in Samsung, Google and Fitbit. Steps are one de-duplicated daily total, never added up across sources. Clients can correct a wrong value. Finished workouts are written back to Apple Health and Health Connect.
**Already does it:** Hevy (watch apps, writes workouts back), Harbiz (direct Garmin, Fitbit and Oura), PT Distinction (Apple Watch).

### P7. Food logging is slow and the database is wrong outside the US
97 mentions (57).
> "not only is it US-centric, but the serving sizes are incredibly inconsistent." (Everfit)

**Infinilift:** a built-in food diary with no MyFitnessPal needed. Australian foods come first (FSANZ data plus Open Food Facts barcodes), and checked entries rank above ones users added. Recents and favourites sit at the top, decimals work, and clients can log to any day. If a barcode misses, the client scans the label and it's saved for everyone. Fibre, sugar and sodium are tracked. The coach can hide the numbers for clients who shouldn't count.
**Already does it:** Coachway (misses saved for everyone, a photo-only mode), Foodzilla (AU and NZ data), Easy Diet Diary (Australian database and barcodes).

### P8. Features missing on some screens
184 mentions (30).
> "it's basically only useful for communication. Everything else you'll have to use the website." (Everfit coach app)

**Infinilift:** every coach and client feature works on web, iPhone, Android and tablets. A feature isn't done until it's been checked on all three.
**Already does part of it:** My PT Hub (most coach jobs on the phone, clients on the web), Hevy (one account on phone, web and watch).

### P9. Video fails both ways
105 mentions (37).
> "I easily spend double the time of my workout manipulating video to meet the restrictions of your app" (Everfit)

**Infinilift:** form-check videos are shrunk on the phone and upload in the background, surviving a closed app and weak signal. Clips can run 3 minutes or more. The coach replies with a note pinned to a moment in the video, or a video. Demo videos play inside the app and loop, with no YouTube sign-in.
**Already does part of it:** CoachNow (form-check video), LVLUP (notes pinned to a moment), FitPros.io (demos inside the app).

### P10. Messages and notifications go missing
151 mentions (22).
> "I have missed really important messages from clients multiple times because the entire notification system just stops" (Trainerize)

**Infinilift:** live chat that shows sending, sent and read, retries by itself and keeps drafts. One thread per client; a comment on a set shows up there as a card. Tapping a notification opens the message. Clients can mute promotions without muting their coach, and automated messages are labelled as automated.
**Already does it:** Kahunas (read ticks, threads, 15-minute voice notes), Coachway (one thread per client with check-in cards, transcribed voice notes), PT Distinction (shows messages that failed to send).

### P11. Clients can't see what they lifted last time
62 mentions (26).
> "we should not have to tell the client to go hunt for what they lifted their last session" (Everfit)

**Infinilift:** every set shows last time for that exercise, from any workout, next to today's target. Today's numbers are filled in from the coach's progression rule. Full history, charts, estimated 1RM and PBs as they happen. Notes per exercise carry forward, for example seat height.
**Already does it:** Hevy (last time from any workout), FitPros.io (one-tap fill, instant PBs), LVLUP (auto-progression).

### P12. Coaches pay extra for the basics
25 store mentions (9), and the main coach complaint on Capterra and G2.
> "If you want full branding—your logo and brand name without EverFit's name—it costs an outrageous $4,000-$5,000." (Everfit)

**Infinilift:** one plan with the basics included: nutrition, automation, payments, check-ins, video and branding. The coach's own training account and archived clients don't use client slots. Payments go to the coach's own Stripe account.
**Already does it:** Kahunas (unlimited clients and an own-name app for US$99/mo), PT Distinction (every feature on every plan), My PT Hub (unlimited clients for US$59/mo).

### P13. Rest and interval timers are unreliable
68 mentions (27).
> "the rest timer never goes off unless you keep the app open and awake for the whole duration." (Everfit)

**Infinilift:** the rest timer starts when a set is ticked, stays on screen and goes off on time with the phone locked or another app open (lock-screen timer on iPhone and Android). The coach's rest time is the default, with ±15 s buttons. Interval, EMOM, AMRAP and timed sections run on a built-in clock. Sounds play over the client's music instead of stopping it, and demo videos start muted.
**Already does part of it:** Hevy (automatic timer on the lock screen).

### P14. Support can't be reached
64 mentions (19).
> "When your app stops working you're told to contact your trainer by email or msg, using the app, which you can't access!!!!" (Trainerize)

**Infinilift:** "Report a problem" on every screen, and on shake. It works logged out and offline and attaches the device and app version. App problems go to platform support; training questions go to the coach. Public status page.
**Already does it:** Kahunas (shake to report, public roadmap), Practice Better (public status page).

### P15. Clients can't cancel or keep their data
97 mentions (12). 57 of them are one 2024 trial trap in a Trainerize-hosted app, but the pattern is in all three apps.
> "after my time with my trainer ended, they immediately locked me out and I have no way to retrieve my information" (Trainerize)

**Infinilift:** clients manage and cancel their own plan without asking the coach. Checkout shows the exact charge, renewal date and trial end up front, with nothing pre-ticked. When coaching ends, the client keeps their history and can download it.
**Already does it:** Hevy (client owns the account, keeps history, exports CSV), FITR (training stays forever), Coachway (access after cancelling is a coach setting).

No app we found fully fixes P1, P3, P4, P9 or P13.

## What users love (keep it)

- Programs laid out with sets, reps, rest and a demo video, so clients who don't know the gym can follow along.
- Last session's numbers filled in, PBs tracked and a nudge to add weight.
- Chat with the coach plus comments on each workout, with the coach seeing everything logged.
- The form-check loop: the client films a set, the coach replies with video.
- A fast desktop program builder: calendar, copy and paste, drag and drop, keyboard shortcuts, supersets, AMRAP and intervals.
- A simple client app that beginners and older clients can use.
- A daily to-do list of workouts, habits and tasks, with streaks.
- Progress photos and measurements side by side, which keep clients going when the scales stall.
- Celebrations: PB animations, badges and a yearly recap.
- Coach triage: compliance rates, "needs attention" flags, and client types for remote, in-person and both.
- Pay-and-onboard automation, so a client can buy and start with no manual steps.
- Importing a coach's old programs from text or PDF, so switching takes minutes.
