# The 9 coaching platforms

Researched 11 October 2026 from each vendor's website, pricing page, help centre and store listings, and from reviews on the App Store, Google Play, Capterra, G2, Trustpilot and Reddit. A second researcher checked each report. Prices are what each vendor shows, before GST.

User pain points from about 2,700 store reviews are in `pain-points.md`.

## ABC Trainerize

**What it is.** The biggest. Started in Vancouver in 2012 and is now owned by ABC Fitness (Texas). Claims 400,000+ trainers and 6 million clients.

**How it works** (help centre):

1. Clients > New: pick a client type (full access, offline or a free basic client) and send the invite.
2. Master Libraries > Programs > New: set phases, build each phase's workouts, then assign the program.
3. Client profile > Goals > Daily Nutrition Goal: calories, calories and protein, or full macros. Clients track in the app or through MyFitnessPal.
4. Forms > New: build a check-in (owner or admin only) and schedule it on the calendar.
5. Payments: buy the Stripe add-on, pick the country and currency (it can't be changed later), make products and set them to onboard a client automatically on purchase.

**Good.** All-in-one at scale. Phased programs with a progression spreadsheet. Pay-and-onboard automation. The widest wearable list, with a mature Apple Watch app.

**Falls short.**
- Lost workouts and crashes after updates. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fitness-app-abc-trainerize/id516851502))
- Watch and wearable sync is unreliable. Samsung Health was missing for years.
- Costs stack up: client tiers plus add-ons for payments, nutrition, video and branding. ([pricing](https://www.trainerize.com/pricing/))
- The cheaper branded app is Android-only; iPhone clients still download "Fitness App (ABC Trainerize)". Builds take 12 to 16 weeks.
- No real data export. 11 outages listed on ABC's status page from February to August 2026.

## TrueCoach

**What it is.** The simplest. Boulder, Colorado, founded 2015 as Fitbot, now part of Xplor. Claims 16,000+ coaches and 250,000+ clients. Coaches in the US, UK, Australia and Canada only.

**How it works:**

1. Add Client: remote (gets an invite), dual (the coach can also log) or in-person (no login).
2. Programs: build days on a calendar with copy, paste, drag and keyboard shortcuts; exercise names link library videos.
3. Nutrition: set calorie, macro and fibre goals and upload meal-plan PDFs. Clients log totals or sync totals from MyFitnessPal.
4. Check-ins: there's no form builder. Coaches write the questions as "exercises" in a workout.
5. Payments: connect Stripe, create a product and plan, then a subscription.

**Good.** The fastest program builder. Clean client app (4.86 stars from 35,595 US ratings). Video form checks with video replies. Support rated 4.8.

**Falls short.**
- Clients log results in a free-text box, so there are no structured sets or progress charts. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/truecoach/id1439127794))
- No food diary or database; MyFitnessPal totals only.
- No check-in forms, booking, storefront or community. Automation only through Zapier.
- "Branded app" is a logo and colour inside the TrueCoach app.
- A flat 5% on every payment, refunds included, against Stripe's 1.65% + A$0.30 on Australian cards.
- Clients can't export their own workouts, and archived clients can't log in at all.

## Everfit

**What it is.** The fastest-moving. San Francisco, founded 2019. Claims 380,000+ coaches.

**How it works:**

1. Add Client (single, several or CSV), then invite by email or link.
2. Build workouts by drag and drop, or paste text or a PDF into the AI Workout Builder. Programs go in the Master Planner and are assigned with a start date.
3. Client profile > Macros > Set Goal, by hand or auto-calculated, with rest-day macros.
4. Forms > Create, then add a repeating task (form, body metrics or progress photo) to the client.
5. Payments need the Payment & Packages add-on and Stripe onboarding, on the web only. Automation needs the Autoflow add-on.

**Good.** Easy to learn (Capterra ease of use 4.8). The AI import makes switching platforms quick. Ships features quickly.

**Falls short.**
- No client web app, and the coach app can't build programs or onboard clients. ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/202837/Everfit/reviews/))
- Add-on sprawl: automation, payments, meal plans, on-demand, Zapier and AI are each extra.
- Food database is US-centric, and step counts from wearables come in wrong.
- Own-name app only on Enterprise. One coach reports a US$4,000 to US$5,000 quote, and another says Everfit sells add-ons to their clients directly.
- New users see only a login screen, with no way to sign up.

## PT Distinction

**What it is.** UK company started by two brothers in 2010, after one of them moved to Sydney.

**How it works.** A web Trainer Portal plus trainer mode in the app. Packages auto-enrol a client into onboarding and program groups that drip content.

**Good.** Everything, AI included, on every plan. Branded iOS and Android apps at no extra fee from US$59.90/mo. In-app, email and SMS in one place, with transcribed voice notes.

**Falls short.** Steep learning curve and a dated look. The client app crashes and loses workout data, with no offline logging. Clients can't swap an exercise mid-workout. PayPal only (Stripe through Zapier). Email-only support. Most of its reviews date from 2021. ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.ptdistinction.com))

## Kahunas

**What it is.** UAE-registered, hosted in the UK, strong in the UK and Australia.

**How it works.** A web dashboard plus a coach mode in the app. Its own food database (1.6 million foods, 800,000 barcodes across the US, UK, AU and CA) and forms with thresholds that alert the coach.

**Good.** Unlimited clients and an own-name app for US$99/mo, with 0% payment commission. Support and 1:1 onboarding are praised (Trustpilot 4.8 from 459 reviews).

**Falls short.** Crashes mid-workout that lose data, and no offline logging. Nutrition search and barcodes miss. The own-name Android apps sit under Kahunas's Google Play account, the iPhone versions lag the main app, and they must sell through Apple in-app purchase. No importing of past client data. ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/kahunas.io))

## Foodzilla

**What it is.** Auckland. Nutrition-first, for dietitians and nutrition coaches. No workout programming.

**Good.** Meal plans in under a minute with swaps that keep macros. Australian food data (AUSNUT, FSANZ). Per-client toggles that hide numbers for clients who shouldn't count. Check-in forms that write straight into the client's records.

**Falls short.** Nutrition data and portion errors. A 7% platform fee on client payments (Pro). Prices exclude GST. Weak client app ratings. ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/206847/Foodzilla/reviews/))

## LVLUP

**What it is.** Small (2 to 10 staff), registered in Delaware, likely run from the Middle East.

**Good.** Flat price per coach with unlimited clients. One-tap meal swaps that keep macros, and an automatic grocery list.

**Falls short.** Hardly any independent reviews. The own-name app sits under LVLUP's store account, with a 12-month minimum. Conflicting pricing and refund terms. ([pricing](https://lvlup-app.com/pricing))

## FitPros.io

**What it is.** One developer who is also a PT, US-hosted. A web app for coaches and a home-screen web app for clients.

**Good.** Free with unlimited clients. Last session's sets pre-filled, an auto-start rest timer and automatic PRs. "Design one week, auto-progress over 4, 8 or 12 weeks."

**Falls short.** No store app, no nutrition, no booking and no wearables. Depends on one person. ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1087466/FitPros))

## Coachway

**What it is.** Copenhagen, launched December 2025. Nordic and German coaches.

**Good.** The "Power Panel": client list with filters (missing check-in, unread, failed payment, plan ending), chat and client file on one screen. Check-in cards inside the chat. Macro targets per weekday with exceptions. A photo-only food log for clients who shouldn't count numbers.

**Falls short.** Young, with few integrations. No groups, community or booking. iPhone only, with no iPad. Its reviews were incentivised. ([reviews](https://coachway.io/reviews/))
