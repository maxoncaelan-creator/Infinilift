# Before and after examples

The businesses here are fictional. Each example lists its **fuel** (the facts supplied) because every detail in the "after" version comes from the fuel. Nothing is invented.

## 1. Google Business Profile post

**Fuel:** Dave's Plumbing, Penrith NSW. Family business since 2007, Dave and his son Josh. 4.8 stars from 96 Google reviews. Same-day service in Penrith, St Marys and Emu Plains. July offer: $99 hot water system check covering the thermostat, pressure relief valve and anode. Dave answers 0400 000 000. Review quote, used with the customer's OK: "Dave told us our system had years left and didn't try to sell us a new one."

**Before**

> Is your hot water system letting you down this winter? 🥶 Don't let a cold shower ruin your morning! At Dave's Plumbing, our dedicated team of experts is passionate about delivering seamless, hassle-free plumbing solutions to our valued community — so you can enjoy the peace of mind you deserve. Whether you need a quick repair or a full replacement, we've got you covered. Contact us today and say goodbye to cold showers for good! 🔧✨

**After**

> $99 hot water check, all through July.
>
> Dave or Josh will test your system's thermostat, pressure relief valve and anode, then tell you straight whether it has another winter in it. One customer wrote: "Dave told us our system had years left and didn't try to sell us a new one."
>
> Same-day in Penrith, St Marys and Emu Plains. Call 0400 000 000 and Dave will pick up.

**What changed**
- Opens with the offer and price, not a rhetorical question (tell 3, one tidy arc).
- "Dedicated team of experts" became Dave and Josh by name (tells 4 and 8).
- Cut "peace of mind you deserve" and "say goodbye to cold showers" (tells 1, 2 and 9).
- The review quote and "tell you straight" give an honest, specific reason to call (tells 5 and 7).
- Named the suburbs. Removed the emoji and the em dash.

| Blocked drains, same day | 24 |
| 4.8 stars from 96 reviews | 25 |
| $99 winter hot water check | 26 |
| Dave answers the phone | 22 |
| Penrith, St Marys, Emu Plains | 29 |

| Description | Chars |
|---|---|
| Family plumbers in Penrith since 2007. Same-day visits to St Marys and Emu Plains. | 82 |
| $99 hot water check in July. We test the thermostat, relief valve and anode. | 76 |

**What changed**
- Every headline now holds a fact a competitor couldn't copy (tell 5, sameness).
- "Guaranteed" is gone: it's a promise the fuel doesn't support.
- Each headline works on its own, because Google mixes and matches them.

## 3. Homepage hero (health clinic)

**Fuel:** Harbour Physio, Manly NSW. Owner and physio Priya Shah. 2 minutes' walk from Manly Wharf. Open until 7 pm on weekdays and on Saturday mornings. First appointments are 45 minutes. Treats running injuries, back pain and rehab after surgery. HICAPS claims on the spot. Online booking.

**Before**

> Welcome to Harbour Physio, where your health and wellbeing is our top priority. Our passionate team of highly qualified physiotherapists is dedicated to helping you unlock your full potential and live pain-free. Using cutting-edge techniques and a holistic approach, we deliver tailored treatment plans designed to get you back to doing what you love. Don't let pain hold you back. Book today and take the first step on your journey to recovery!

**After**

> **Physio in Manly, 2 minutes from the wharf**
>
> Open until 7 pm on weekdays, and on Saturday mornings. Your first appointment is 45 minutes, so there's time to work out what's actually going on.
>
> Most people see us for running injuries, back pain or rehab after surgery. Claim on the spot with HICAPS.
>
> [Book online]

**What changed**
- Removed "live pain-free": AHPRA's advertising rules don't allow copy that creates unreasonable expectations of results.
- No testimonials: health practitioners can't use them in advertising.
- The headline is the place and the landmark, not a welcome (tell 4).
- Cut "unlock your full potential", "holistic" and "journey to recovery" (tells 1 and 6).
- The 45-minute detail replaces "tailored treatment plans". It shows the care instead of claiming it.

## 4. Cold email from Alio!

**Fuel:** To Maria, owner of Bean There Café, Newtown (email published on the café's website). Alio! has recorded a 5-minute video audit. Findings: the Google profile says closed on Sundays, but the café's Instagram says open 8 am to 2 pm on Sundays. 3 reviews from the last month have no reply. The café shows 5th on Google Maps for "cafe newtown".

**Before**

> Subject: Unlock Your Café's Full Potential! 🚀
>
> Hi there, I hope this email finds you well! In today's competitive digital landscape, having a strong online presence is more important than ever. At Alio! Brand Studios, we're passionate about helping businesses like yours elevate their brand and drive growth. Our comprehensive suite of services — from stunning websites to cutting-edge SEO — is designed to take your business to the next level. Would you be open to a quick chat to explore how we can help you achieve your goals? Looking forward to hearing from you!

**After**

> Subject: Your Google profile says you're closed on Sundays
>
> Hi Maria,
>
> I'm [YOUR NAME] from Alio! Brand Studios. I help local businesses get more calls and bookings from Google.
>
> Your Google profile lists Bean There as closed on Sundays, but your Instagram says 8 to 2. So anyone searching "cafe newtown" on a Sunday morning is told you're shut. You're also 5th on Google Maps for that search, and 3 recent reviews haven't had a reply.
>
> I recorded a 5-minute video showing where each of these is and how to fix them yourself. Want me to send the link?
>
> [YOUR NAME]
> Alio! Brand Studios · [PHONE]
>
> Not interested? Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.

**What changed**
- The subject line is her problem, specific enough that only she could receive it (tell 5).
- "In today's competitive digital landscape" and every other speech is gone (tell 6).
- One person talking to one person, in first person (tell 8).
- One small ask, with a free, useful thing attached. No "take your business to the next level" (tell 9).
- Says who is writing and has an opt-out, as the Spam Act requires.

## 5. Monthly report, slow month

**Fuel:** Dave's Plumbing. Calls from Google: 41 in May, 33 in June. Last year: 38 in May, 29 in June. From 10 June a competitor, Pipe Pros, started Google Ads on "blocked drain penrith". Done in June: added 12 photos, replied to 5 reviews, fixed Saturday hours. Proposal for July: a blocked-drain ad campaign at $15 a day, needs the client's approval.

**Before**

> June was a month of growth and learning for Dave's Plumbing. While calls experienced a slight dip, this is a natural part of the seasonal ebb and flow of business. We remain committed to optimising your online presence and are confident that the strategic foundations we've laid will drive robust results moving forward. Rest assured, we're working hard behind the scenes to ensure your continued success!

**After**

> Calls from Google fell from 41 in May to 33 in June.
>
> Two reasons. June is usually quiet for you: last year calls dropped from 38 to 29. And from 10 June, Pipe Pros started running ads on "blocked drain penrith", which pushes your listing further down the page.
>
> What I did in June: added 12 photos, replied to 5 reviews and fixed your Saturday hours.
>
> What I'd like to try in July: a small blocked-drain ad campaign at $15 a day, so you show up above Pipe Pros. Reply "yes" and I'll set it up.

**What changed**
- The first line is the number, bad news included (Alio! principle: honest, always).
- "Seasonal ebb and flow" became last year's actual figures (tell 4).
- Names the competitor and the date instead of vague reassurance (tells 4 and 7).
- "Rest assured, we're working hard behind the scenes" became a list of what was done and one clear ask (tells 1 and 9).
