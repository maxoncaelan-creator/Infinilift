# Security

Please don't open a public issue for a security problem. Email caelan@aliobrandstudio.com with what you found and the steps to repeat it.

Infinilift holds health information: body measurements, progress photos, injuries, food logs and wearable data. Australian privacy law treats it as sensitive information, so:

- Staging and local use fake data only.
- Client data never goes into issues, PRs, logs, screenshots or AI prompts outside the app.
- Keys and passwords live in GitHub, EAS and Supabase settings, never in the repo.
